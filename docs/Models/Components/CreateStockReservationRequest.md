# CreateStockReservationRequest

Hold stock for a holder so it stops counting as available while a checkout completes. Required: `holder_type` (a lowercase short name you choose for the kind of holder — letters, digits and underscores, up to 40 characters, e.g. `storefront_cart`), `holder_id` (the public ID of that holder, UUID) and `lines[]` (1 to 100 entries). Each line requires `product_id` and a `quantity` greater than zero, with up to 10 integer digits and 4 decimals; it may also carry `product_variant_id`, `warehouse_id` (an active warehouse of the company in the path — omitted, the warehouse planned for that product is used) and `holder_line_id` to match the hold back to a line of your own cart. `expires_in_seconds` is optional and is a DURATION counted from the moment the server handles the request, between 1 and 86400; omit it for a hold that never expires. An absolute expiry date is not accepted, so a clock skew on your side can never decide the hold lifetime. The origin of the hold is never taken from the body: it is stamped by the platform. The creation is all-or-nothing — if a single line asks for more than the available quantity, nothing is held and the response reports the requested and the available amount per line.
