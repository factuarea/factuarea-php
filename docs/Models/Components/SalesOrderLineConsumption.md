# SalesOrderLineConsumption

One consumption of a sales order line: the document that took a quantity away from it, and how much. The sum of the consumptions of one concept matches EXACTLY the corresponding quantity of the line. It publishes neither the identity of the allocation nor the instant it was recorded: an integrator does not operate on allocations, they move by registering or reverting the consumption of a document.
