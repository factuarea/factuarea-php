# BulkResolveStorefrontAvailabilityRequest

Ask, for a whole cart and in ONE request, how many units of each line a shopper can still buy: the PHYSICAL quantity minus the quantity COMMITTED by live reservations, never the physical balance. `selections` is required, needs at least one entry and is capped at the declared ceiling of 50. Each entry takes the SAME selection shape as the price resolution — `product_id` required, plus the variant, the presentation, the configuration, the signature, `options` and `quantity` — so a storefront can send the same cart to both operations without rewriting it. And, written here rather than left to be discovered: the units are counted on the ARTICLE and its VARIANT, so the presentation, the configuration, the chosen options and the quantity are accepted to let the cart travel whole and do NOT change the answer. `quantity` is optional here, because what is published is what is available and not whether what you asked for fits. The answer carries one entry per index of the input, in the same order, and a line pointing at an article of another shop is reported as not found with its index while the rest answer normally. No cost, no supplier, no reorder point, no committed quantity, no physical balance and no per-warehouse breakdown ever travel in the answer.
