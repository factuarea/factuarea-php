# StockTransferStatusItem

A stock transfer status from the closed catalog, with its label and what it allows. Returned by `GET /v1/companies/{company}/stock-transfers/statuses` as a flat list. The catalog enumerates STATUSES and not transition targets: `draft` is in it and is the target of no transition at all.
