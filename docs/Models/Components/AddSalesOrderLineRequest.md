# AddSalesOrderLineRequest

Add ONE line to a sales order that still accepts changes. The line is born with its ordered quantity; the served, invoiced and returned quantities are never sent here or by any other route — they move on their own when a delivery note, an invoice or a return consumes quantity from this line. The subject of the line is either a catalog product (`product_id`) or free text: with no `product_id`, `description` and `unit_price` become required, and with one, a price you send WINS over the catalog price while omitting it lets the catalog resolve it with the rate that applies to the buyer. `quantity` is required, greater than zero and with at most four decimals. `discount`, `vat_rate`, `retention_rate` and `surcharge_rate` are percentages between 0 and 100, and `vat_id`, `retention_id` and `surcharge_id` point at the tax catalog. `warehouse_id` is the warehouse this line ships from; omitting it inherits the one on the order. The field names are the SAME ones each element of `lines` uses when creating or updating an order, and the same ones the lines sub-resource returns: a line is written the same way by both routes. Beyond the ceiling of each field, the line amount and the taxes derived from it have their own, narrower limit: a combination of price, quantity and percentage whose result overflows it is rejected naming the amount that does not fit and its value, even when price and quantity are legitimate on their own. An order that no longer accepts changes rejects the creation of lines as a business-rule violation, not as a validation error.
