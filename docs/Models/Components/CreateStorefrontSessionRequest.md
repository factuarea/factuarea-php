# CreateStorefrontSessionRequest

Open a cart session for a shopper and get back its public id, which is the ONLY key to that cart: whoever holds it can read it and change it, so it is unguessable and has to be treated as a secret. The cart may be born EMPTY — `lines` is optional and an empty list is valid, because opening a cart and filling it are two different moments — and the number of lines is capped at the declared ceiling of 50. Each line carries `product_id` (required), the variant, the presentation, the configuration and the signature when there are any, the `options` chosen as pairs of `group_id` and `value_id`, and the `quantity`, greater than zero and with at most four decimals. THE PRICE IS NOT SENT: this body accepts no amount field and any amount that arrives is IGNORED, because the line stores the CHOICE and the server resolves the amount of every line and keeps it with the moment it was resolved. The price list is not chosen from the browser either. The buyer is optional but is given WHOLE: as soon as any of its fields travels, the minimum to serve and to notify becomes required — `buyer_name`, `buyer_email` and the shipping address (`shipping_address_line`, `shipping_city` and `shipping_country`) —, because half an identity serves neither to deliver nor to warn. The tax id is OPTIONAL, since a private individual may not have one. The consent to commercial communications does not travel here: it is a field of the ORDER, declared when it is confirmed. Neither the IP address of the shopper nor any browser tracking identifier is stored. The cart expires after the lifetime configured for the storefront, 24 hours by default, and once expired it admits no changes and cannot be confirmed.
