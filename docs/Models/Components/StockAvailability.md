# StockAvailability

What can still be SOLD of an item: the physical balance minus what reservations are holding. It is the figure a storefront has to read — publishing the physical one oversells, because units already committed to a paid order would be offered again. An item that does not manage stock publishes its three totals as `null` and not as zero: zero is a figure and reads as `sold out`.
