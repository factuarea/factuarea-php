# ReceiveStockTransferRequest

Receive the goods of a dispatched stock transfer, in full or in part. `lines` is required and needs at least one entry: each one names the transfer line being received (`stock_transfer_line_id`, the public ID the lines sub-resource returns) and the `quantity` received NOW — not the running total —, greater than zero and with at most four decimals. The same line may not be declared twice in one call, not even written in a different case: the second entry would overwrite the first and the goods declared in it would vanish with nothing to show for it. Receiving ADDS to what that line had already received, so the operation may be repeated until everything dispatched has arrived, and a quantity that takes a line above what was dispatched is rejected as a business-rule violation. That is also why the operation is irreversible and honours an idempotency key: a second identical call without one would advance the received quantity twice.
