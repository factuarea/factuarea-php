# GoodsReceipt

A goods receipt: the entry of goods of a purchase order into the warehouse. It is registered against an order, it may be PARTIAL and repeatable, and what actually moves the stock is POSTING it — a receipt that is merely created has not moved a single unit. Quantities are decimal strings with four decimal places; this family publishes no amounts, because it counts units and not money.
