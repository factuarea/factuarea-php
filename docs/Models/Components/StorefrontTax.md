# StorefrontTax

The tax that applies to an item of the shop window, as the buyer needs it to render a price: its opaque identifier, its name and its rate. Its three keys ALWAYS travel, `null` on all three when the item carries no tax: "this item has no tax" is a fact about the item and not the absence of a field.
