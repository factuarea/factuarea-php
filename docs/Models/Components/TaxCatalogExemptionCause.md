# TaxCatalogExemptionCause

An exemption, non-subjection or special-regime cause, with the AEAT code it projects to, the legal wording the invoice must carry and the article of the Spanish VAT Act (Ley 37/1992) it relies on. The three AEAT codes are mutually exclusive: every cause derives at most one of them, and the other two are `null`.
