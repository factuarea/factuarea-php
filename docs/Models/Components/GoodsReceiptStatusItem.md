# GoodsReceiptStatusItem

A goods receipt status from the closed catalog, with its label, its colour and what it allows. Returned by `GET /v1/companies/{company}/goods-receipts/statuses` as a flat list. It publishes the same seven keys as the status catalog of the other document families, so a client models one shape and not six.
