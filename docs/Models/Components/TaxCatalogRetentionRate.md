# TaxCatalogRetentionRate

A system withholding rate offered by the platform. Most entries are IRPF withholdings, but not all of them, and the `IRPF_` prefix of the `code` is no proof otherwise — codes are stable identifiers, not a tax classification. Two entries are not IRPF: `IRPF_5_GARANTIA` is contractual retention money on a construction contract, with no tax nature at all (it is not reported on form 111 and nothing is paid over to AEAT), and `IRPF_24` belongs to IRNR (RDL 5/2004), the non-resident income tax. Read each `label` for the nature of its own rate instead of assuming every entry is an IRPF withholding. This block is the catalog of what Factuarea ships, NOT an exhaustive list of every rate Spanish law defines, and it never includes the custom taxes a company creates through `POST /v1/taxes`.
