# Carrier

A carrier: the company that physically moves the goods of a delivery note. It is a MASTER record and not a document — no number, no series and no lifecycle beyond being active or withdrawn. It is what turns the free-text transport company that used to be typed on every delivery note into a referenced entity, and what makes the tracking URL of a shipment DERIVABLE instead of stored.
