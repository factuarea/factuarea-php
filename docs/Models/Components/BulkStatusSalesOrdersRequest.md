# BulkStatusSalesOrdersRequest

Transition several sales orders to `new_status` (`confirmed`, `cancelled` or `closed`) in one request, up to 100 per batch. `ids` is an array of sales-order UUIDs. Only those three are valid targets: the four consumption states (`partially_served`, `served`, `partially_invoiced`, `invoiced`) are derived from delivery notes and invoices against the order lines and are never requested, and `draft` is never a target because confirming stamps a series number. Every transition passes the document state guard, and orders that cannot transition are returned under `failures[]`. Confirming consumes one series number per confirmed order.
