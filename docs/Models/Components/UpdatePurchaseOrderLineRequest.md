# UpdatePurchaseOrderLineRequest

Partial update of ONE line of a purchase order that still accepts changes. Every field is optional and an empty body is valid: it changes nothing and is not an error. Of a purchase line you edit the QUANTITY, and only the quantity: `quantity` is the new ordered quantity in PURCHASE UNIT — the one published by the offer the line was copied from — greater than zero, with at most four decimals and up to 9999999999.9999; it does not accept `null`, because a line with no quantity does not exist, and to leave it as it is you simply omit it. Changing the product, the variant or the offer is NOT editing a line: it is removing it and adding another. The line keeps the agreed terms — supplier reference, purchase unit, conversion factor, cost, minimum quantity and lead time — and they are copied ALL AT ONCE from a single offer only when the line is added; accepting a new product here would leave a line with the unit of one offer and the cost of another. That is why `product_id`, `product_variant_id` and `offer_id` are rejected naming the field, with the alternative in the message, instead of being ignored. With the same criterion this body rejects the unit cost (`unit_cost` and `unit_price`) and the three percentages — the ones of the copied offer —, the `description` — composed from the product —, the `warehouse_id` — fixed when the line is added —, the line `id` — which is the one in the URL, not a field of the body — and the `received_quantity`, which is never writable on any route. The line amount and the taxes derived from it have their own limit, checked against the cost the line already holds before the change is applied. Lowering the quantity below what has already been received, or editing a line of an order whose status no longer allows changes, is rejected as a business-rule violation and not as a validation error.
