# StockTransferLine

A line of a stock transfer: ONE item travelling between the two warehouses. It carries QUANTITIES and no amount, and the three of them are decimal strings with FOUR decimal places — the scale of the quantities of the catalog, never the two-decimal scale of money, because a transfer has no price to round.
