# CreateWarehouseRequest

Create a warehouse in the company master data. Required: `code`, `name`, `type` (`own`, `external`, `store` or `transit`) and the `address` object (`line1`, `city`, `province`, `postal_code`; `line2` and `country` are optional and `country` defaults to `ES`). The `code` is the integration key: up to 20 characters of letters, digits and hyphens, stored upper-cased and trimmed, and unique among the LIVE warehouses of the same company — a duplicate returns 422 pointing at `code`, never a 409 creation conflict. Two different companies may each use the same code, and archiving a warehouse frees its code for reuse. The optional `is_default` flag marks the new warehouse as the company default, unmarking the previous one in the same write.
