# CreateCompanyV1Request

Register a tax ID (NIF) in your account. `name` and `tax_id` are required; the rest of the profile (business name, fiscal address, contact details) is optional. `tax_id` is validated as a Spanish tax ID (NIF/CIF/NIE) and must be unique within your account; `country_aeat_zone` is derived from the postal code. The new company joins your account and is adopted into the scope of the calling key, so you can operate on it right away; it is NOT linked to any accounting-firm hierarchy. A tax ID already registered in another account is rejected and has to be claimed instead.
