# StorefrontProduct

The full sheet of an item of the shop window: the fourteen keys of its row plus its four collections —variants, presentations, option groups and gallery— so a product page can be painted with ONE read instead of five. The four collections carry exactly what the sub-collection operations return, so reading the sheet and reading its parts can never disagree.
