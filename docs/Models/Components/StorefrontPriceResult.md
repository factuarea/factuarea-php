# StorefrontPriceResult

One entry of a batch price resolution, addressed by the INDEX of the selection that was sent. The batch answers a whole cart in one request and a selection that cannot be resolved does NOT invalidate the rest: it comes back in its own entry with its reason, and the others carry their price. The index is the only thing that ties an entry to the line the caller sent, which is why the answer never comes back compacted.
