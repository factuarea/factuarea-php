# FulfilmentStatusItem

A status of the PHYSICAL axis of a delivery note, from the closed catalog, with its label and its colour token. Returned by `GET /v1/companies/{company}/delivery-notes/fulfilment-statuses` as a flat list. The three fields are DERIVED from the single source of the axis, never copied into a hand-written list. This catalog and the one of delivery note statuses are TWO different catalogs published by two different operations: they are orthogonal axes, and mixing them would let a filter of one accept values of the other. No value of this catalog appears in that one.
