# ConsolidatedWorkforce

A row of the consolidated workforce compliance panel of an account: the public identity of one `active` tax ID plus its time-tracking compliance summary, projected from that company’s latest monthly close without recomputation. A computed resource with no identity of its own: its `id` is the UUID v7 of the tax ID, never a record id.
