# ReturnLine

One line of a return: ONE item coming back, with the quantity and the reason. The unit price and the tax rate are INHERITED from the line of the origin document and are never accepted from the client: the amount that will be corrected is not decided by whoever asks for the return, and a corrective invoice that VeriFactu will not let you delete hangs on it.
