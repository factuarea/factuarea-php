# ReturnableLine

One line of an origin document that can still be returned, with how much of it is left. It is the PRE-FLIGHT read of the request: it answers "what can I send back, and how much" BEFORE asking for the return, so a client does not discover the ceiling as a rejection. It is a projection of the origin document and NOT a resource with a life of its own: it has no identity in our system, no lifecycle and no operations.
