# StorefrontBuyerOrder

The order as the BUYER is shown it in their own window, opened with the public link the seller gave them. It is the SAME sales order the merchant works with, published with the eleven keys the buyer is allowed to see: what they ordered, what it costs, where the order stands, where its collection stands and which invoice was issued for it. The identity of the buyer is NOT republished here —whoever opens the window already knows who they are, and repeating their fiscal identity and their addresses on every read would put them in a response the browser caches for nothing—, and neither is the status of the public link, which is what the SERVER uses to decide whether to serve the order at all.
