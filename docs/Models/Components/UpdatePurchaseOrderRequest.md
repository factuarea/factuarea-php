# UpdatePurchaseOrderRequest

Partial update of the HEADER of a purchase order. Every field is optional and an empty body is valid: it changes nothing and is not an error. What you do not send keeps its current value and what you send as `null` is CLEARED, which is how you remove the expected date or the destination warehouse without touching anything else. Writable: `supplier_order_reference`, `warehouse_id`, `expected_date` and `notes`. What is not writable here: the SUPPLIER, which is the identity of the order — buying from somebody else is another order, not an edit of this one —; the LINES, which have their own sub-resource, so adding, changing or removing one does not mean resending the rest; the NUMBER, stamped at creation; and the STATUS, which the transition operations move. Reusing a `supplier_order_reference` another order of your company already holds is rejected with a conflict, exactly like at creation. An order whose status no longer admits editing rejects this operation as a business-rule violation, with 422 and never 403.
