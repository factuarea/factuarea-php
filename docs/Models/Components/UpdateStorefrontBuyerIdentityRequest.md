# UpdateStorefrontBuyerIdentityRequest

PARTIAL update of the fiscal identity of the buyer of an order: every field is optional, what you do not send is NOT touched, and an empty body is a valid request that changes nothing. It is the call a storefront makes when the order needs a full invoice and the buyer has to complete what the cart did not ask for. `buyer_tax_id` is the fiscal identification, and the billing block is street, town, province, postal code and two-letter country; `full_invoice_requested` says whether the buyer is asking for a full invoice instead of a simplified one. Country becomes required as soon as any other field of the billing address travels: an address with no country can neither be invoiced nor validated. Completing the identity is what PROMOTES the buyer to a business contact of the seller —reused by tax id when one already exists— and the response returns the updated order together with the reference of that contact, or `null` when nobody was promoted. An order that no longer admits collection rejects the call, and an order of another shop answers as not found.
