# StorefrontRevalidationLine

The verdict on one line of the cart after revalidating it: what it cost before, what it costs now, whether there are still enough units and whether the combination can still be offered. The three axes are published apart because they fail apart: a line can have gone up in price and still fit, and it can still be cheap and no longer be offered at all.
