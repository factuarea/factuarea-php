# SalesOrder

A sales order: the commitment to serve goods or services to a buyer, and the document the delivery notes and invoices that fulfil it are derived from. It is NOT a fiscal document, so it only takes a number from its series when it is confirmed and `number` stays `null` while it is a draft. Amounts are decimal strings with two decimal places; line quantities are decimal strings with four.
