# UpdateSalesOrderRequest

Partial update of a sales order. Every field is optional and an empty body is valid: what you do not send keeps its current value and what you send as `null` is CLEARED, which is the point of this operation — an update that only changes the notes cannot wipe the expected delivery date along the way. Writable: `order_date`, `expected_delivery_date`, `series_id`, `warehouse_id`, `business_contact_id`, `external_id`, `notes` and `lines[]`. `lines` is the COMPLETE collection and REPLACES the current one: it is a replacement and not a merge, so a line missing from the list stops being part of the order, and every line you want to keep must carry its `id` (the public line identifier the order returns on read) — a line with no `id` is created. What is not writable here: the origin channel is fixed at creation; the status is moved by the confirm, cancel and close operations, and the service and invoicing states are DERIVED from the documents that consume the order; the number is stamped by the confirmation; and the buyer identity and the shipping address each have their own operation, because changing the buyer drags the billing address along and changing where the goods go must not reopen who bought them. An order whose status no longer allows editing rejects this operation as a business-rule violation.
