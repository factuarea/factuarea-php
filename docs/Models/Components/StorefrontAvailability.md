# StorefrontAvailability

What the buyer is allowed to know about the stock of an item: ONE figure, the available total. It is the physical stock MINUS what live reservations already hold, so it is what can still be sold and not what is in the building. There is NO breakdown per warehouse, and its absence is the contract and not an omission: how many warehouses a merchant has and how the goods are spread across them is information about the merchant, and this credential travels in the browser of an anonymous visitor. Neither the physical quantity, nor the committed one, nor the reorder point, nor the cost, nor the supplier are published.
