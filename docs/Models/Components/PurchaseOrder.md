# PurchaseOrder

A purchase order: the commitment to buy goods from a supplier, and the document the goods receipts and the supplier invoices that fulfil it are matched against. It is NOT a fiscal document, but unlike a sales order it takes its number when it is CREATED, so `number` is always present — even on a draft. Amounts are decimal strings with two decimal places; line quantities are decimal strings with four.
