# ManagedAccountImportPreview

Preview, with NO effect, of a bulk import: every row with its status, its column errors, its plan and its amounts, the totals by status and the cost by mode. Nothing is written and nothing is charged. Amounts are integer CENTS, taxable base without VAT; the key names the unit (`*_cents`). Applying the same files charges the prorated total announced here.
