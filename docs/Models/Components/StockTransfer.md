# StockTransfer

A stock transfer: goods moving from one warehouse of the company to another. It is NOT a fiscal document —it has no series, no customer and no amounts— and it carries no price at all: its lines count units. Dispatching it books the stock OUT of the origin and receiving it books the stock IN at the destination, so the ledger of the product shows both legs; cancelling it moves nothing.
