# UpdateSalesOrderLineRequest

Partial update of ONE line of a sales order. Every field is optional: send the ones that change, and the ones you leave out keep the value the line already has, so changing a quantity does not force you to resend the whole line. `product_id` sent as `null` turns the line into free text; `quantity` is the only field that does not accept `null`, because a line with no quantity does not exist; `unit_price` sent as `null` makes the price resolve from the catalog again. `warehouse_id` travels in the BODY and not as one more level of the URL: it is a field of the line like any other, and nesting a third resource just for it would break the clients generated from this contract. What this body never changes: the served, invoiced and returned quantities are preserved and cannot be written, and lowering the ordered quantity below what has already been served or invoiced is rejected as a business-rule violation with the available quantity in the message. The line amount and the taxes derived from it have their own limit, narrower than the ceiling of each field, and a combination that overflows it is rejected naming the amount that does not fit. An order that no longer accepts changes rejects every edit of its lines as a business-rule violation, not as a validation error.
