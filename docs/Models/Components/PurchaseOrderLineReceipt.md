# PurchaseOrderLineReceipt

One reception of a purchase order line: the goods receipt that took a quantity away from it, and how much. Only POSTED receipts appear here — a cancelled one gave the quantity back and counting it would say the goods are in the warehouse when they are not. The sum of these quantities matches EXACTLY the `received_quantity` of the line.
