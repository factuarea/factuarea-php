# BulkResolveStorefrontPricesRequest

Resolve the price of a WHOLE cart in one request, which is the operation to build a storefront on: resolving line by line would be one request per line from the browser of EVERY shopper, and the admission budget of the lane counts them all. `selections` is required, needs at least one entry and is capped at the declared ceiling of 50 — sending more is rejected and resolves nothing. Each entry has the same shape as the single resolution: `product_id` required, then `variant_id`, `presentation_id`, `configuration_id`, `selection_signature`, `options` as pairs of `group_id` and `value_id`, and `quantity`. The answer carries one entry PER INDEX of the input and in the same order, so an entry that cannot be resolved comes back with its index and its reason and does not invalidate the rest of the cart. As in the single resolution the price list is not chosen from the browser, the amounts are a preview the order resolves again, and nothing is stored.
