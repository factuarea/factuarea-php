# ManagedAccountPlanChangePreview

Preview, with no effect, of changing the plan of a managed account your account pays. Amounts are integer CENTS, taxable base without VAT; the key names the unit (`*_cents`). A managed account that is not charged to your account has no real charge: both amounts are 0 and the change is immediate.
