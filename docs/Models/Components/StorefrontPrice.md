# StorefrontPrice

The price of a selection FOR THIS BUYER, resolved by the server. It is the only price that counts: whatever a browser sends as an amount is discarded, and the cart and the order keep what this resolution returned. What the merchant sees behind it —which price list won, the amount of the source, the breakdown of the option adjustments, whether they were absorbed and which resolver answered— is NOT published: it is the commercial policy of the merchant and it would travel in the browser of an anonymous visitor.
