# StorefrontSession

The cart of an anonymous buyer: what they have picked, what it added up to the last time the server priced it, and who they say they are. Its identifier is unguessable and IS the only authorisation over it: whoever holds it can read and change it, and nobody else can. It does NOT hold stock — nothing is set aside for it — so what it shows can stop fitting, and that is what the revalidation is for. Expiry is NOT one of its statuses: it is derived from the expiry instant against the clock of the read, so no scheduled sweep is needed to make a cart stale.
