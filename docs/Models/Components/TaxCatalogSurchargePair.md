# TaxCatalogSurchargePair

A legal pairing of a VAT rate with its equivalence surcharge (recargo de equivalencia). The rates are the ones article 161 of the Spanish VAT Act (Ley 37/1992) fixes; article 154, which anchors the special regime, governs its content and not its rates. The matrix is closed: any other combination is rejected when the invoice line is created. It pairs a surcharge with a VAT rate, so it cannot express the 1.75% rate article 161 reserves for supplies of manufactured tobacco, which depends on the product and not on the VAT rate.
