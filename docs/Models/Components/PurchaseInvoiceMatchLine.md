# PurchaseInvoiceMatchLine

One line of the supplier invoice seen from the three-way match, against its counterpart on the order. Quantities are in BASE units with four decimal places and costs per base unit with six, which is what lets buying in boxes of twelve and invoicing in units NOT read as a price deviation.
