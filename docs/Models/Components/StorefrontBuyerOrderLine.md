# StorefrontBuyerOrderLine

One line of the order as the BUYER is shown it: what was ordered, how much of it, at what unit amount and for what line total. The amount is the one the server FROZE when the order was placed, so later moves of the catalog or of the price list do not rewrite what the buyer agreed to pay. It carries NO catalog identifiers —item, variant, presentation or combination—: the storefront already received them in the response that created the order, and republishing them from this window would widen the surface with nothing asking for it. No cost, no margin, no supplier, no warehouse and no internal key either.
