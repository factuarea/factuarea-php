# MonthlyCloseReport

The monthly report over a closed period for the Control Horario (time tracking) module. A computed resource with no entity identity: it is composed from the frozen snapshot of the close, so it references the close by `close_id` (UUID v7) and never an `id` of its own. The totals never drift from the sheet at the moment of closing. Totals are in minutes.
