# UpdateSalesOrderBuyerRequest

Replace the buyer of a sales order. The buyer is a sub-object of the order and is replaced AS A WHOLE — the body describes who the buyer is now, not which parts of the previous one to touch — so `buyer_name` is required even though the order itself is updated partially, and identity fields you leave out end up empty. `buyer_email` and `buyer_phone` are optional but at least one contact channel is needed; `buyer_tax_id` is optional, because a private individual may not have one. The billing address belongs to the identity and travels with it (`billing_address_line`, `billing_city`, `billing_province`, `billing_postal_code` and `billing_country`, the country a two-letter code that becomes required as soon as any other field of the block is sent). The SHIPPING address is not touched here and has its own operation: an order can change hands and still be delivered to the same place. `business_contact_id` is the one field with three meanings — absent keeps the current link, a public identifier changes it and `null` removes it — and a contact of another company is rejected exactly like a non-existent one. An order whose status no longer allows editing rejects this operation as a business-rule violation.
