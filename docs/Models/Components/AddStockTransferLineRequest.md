# AddStockTransferLineRequest

Add ONE line to a stock transfer that is still a draft. Required: `product_id` and `quantity`, greater than zero, with at most four decimals and up to 9999999999.9999. Optional: `product_variant_id` when one specific variant travels, and `origin_location_id` / `destination_location_id`, the locations inside the origin and the destination warehouse of the transfer; each one is checked against your company by this body and against its own warehouse by the transfer, which is what has both warehouses in front of it. The RECEIVED quantity is rejected naming the field instead of being silently dropped: a line is born with zero received and only the reception moves that number. A transfer whose status no longer admits changes rejects the creation of lines as a business-rule violation, not as a validation error.
