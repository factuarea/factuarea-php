# StorefrontKey

A publishable credential of the shop window: the key the web shop of the merchant carries in the browser of an anonymous visitor to read the catalog and operate the cart. It is PUBLIC by design —anyone who opens the shop can read it— which is why it is fenced by the origins it may be used from, why it only carries the permissions of the buyer lane, and why it can be revoked and rotated without touching anything else. This representation never carries the secret: the secret is shown once, at issuing or after rotation, and never again.
