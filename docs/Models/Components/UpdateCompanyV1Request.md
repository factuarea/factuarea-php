# UpdateCompanyV1Request

Partial update of the profile of a company (tax ID) of your account (business name, fiscal address, contact details). Omitted fields keep their current value; send `""` to clear a field. `country_aeat_zone` is derived from the postal code, and `tax_id` is immutable after creation (sending it returns 422).
