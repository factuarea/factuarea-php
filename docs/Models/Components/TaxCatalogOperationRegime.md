# TaxCatalogOperationRegime

A header-level operation regime: how a transaction is taxed regardless of which indirect tax applies. Three of the four require the invoice to be issued with no VAT amount and to carry a legal wording; sending a rate above zero on those is a fiscal error, not a formatting one.
