# PurchaseOrderStatusItem

A purchase order status from the closed catalog, with its label, its colour and what it allows. Returned by `GET /v1/companies/{company}/purchase-orders/statuses` as a flat list. The three permissions are the contract that spares the integrator a round of trial and error: they say, BEFORE trying, which statuses admit editing the order or deleting it.
