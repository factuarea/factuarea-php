# ResolvePurchaseInvoiceMatchRequest

Body shared by the two decisions that close a three-way match: accepting the deviation it found, and rejecting the match. It carries ONE property, `reason`, the written reason of whoever decides — required, up to 500 characters, it travels with the decision and is what an audit reads afterwards. Accepting leaves the invoice matched, stops it blocking its own status transitions and closes its purchase order as billed, and it is irreversible: no published operation moves a billed purchase order back to an earlier state. Rejecting leaves the invoice blocked on purpose — it can be neither recorded nor paid, and its order is not closed —, which is the state of an invoice that goes back to the supplier.
