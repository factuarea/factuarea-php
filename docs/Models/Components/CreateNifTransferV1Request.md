# CreateNifTransferV1Request

Body of the tax ID transfer issuance. `company_id` is the opaque identifier (UUID v7) of the tax ID of your account that this transfer gives away; it must be within your credential scope and cannot be the owner company of the account. `recipient_account_id` is OPTIONAL and names the only account that will be able to accept the transfer. `expires_at` is OPTIONAL and is a REQUEST, not a command: pass an ISO 8601 instant in the future and within the product ceiling, or omit it and the product default applies. Nothing else is accepted — the issuing account and the acting person come from the authenticated credential, never from the body, because issuing a transfer is giving away control of a tax ID and the giver is always the account that holds it.
