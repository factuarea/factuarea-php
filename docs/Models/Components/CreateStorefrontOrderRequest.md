# CreateStorefrontOrderRequest

Turn what a shopper has in the cart into a real sales order. THE PRICE IS ALWAYS THE SERVER: every line is priced again at creation, ANY AMOUNT PRESENT IN THE BODY IS IGNORED, and neither the amount previewed nor the one stored in the cart session is taken as the price of the line without resolving it again — it is the only real defence against a manipulated browser. The `Idempotency-Key` HEADER IS REQUIRED here and identifies the REQUEST: the same key with the same body returns the SAME order and leaves one single row, and a call without it is rejected. `external_id` is the OTHER axis and the contract keeps the two apart on purpose: it identifies the ORDER in the system that originates it and it is the key of an UPSERT whose uniqueness scope is THE COMPANY — two creations with the same external id inside the same shop return the same order, even from different requests and days apart, while two different companies may each use the same one —, and neither axis is derived from the other. The lines come from one of two mutually exclusive paths: `session_id`, the public id of a cart session, whose lines are the ones the server has stored and are NOT repeated in the body; or `lines`, required when no session is given, with at least one entry and the ceiling of 50. Availability is checked line by line and a single line without enough units rejects the WHOLE order naming that line: no partial order is ever created. The shopper needs `buyer_name`, `buyer_email` and the shipping address (`shipping_address_line`, `shipping_city` and `shipping_country`); `buyer_tax_id` is OPTIONAL, and a shopper without one completes the order, which then creates no business contact at all, because a business contact requires a fiscal identity and a private individual does not have one. `marketing_consent` is REQUIRED and says whether the shopper accepts commercial communications: it is declared instead of presumed in either direction, because silence leaves no record of what the shopper was asked. Neither the IP address of the shopper nor any browser tracking identifier is stored. A session that does not exist, that belongs to another shop, that has expired, that was already turned into an order or that was abandoned rejects the creation saying which of those happened, because the remedy of each one is different. The order is born with the storefront channel, so its origin is distinguishable from a manual entry, from a connected store and from an integrator call.
