# StockReservationStatusItem

A reservation status from the closed catalog, with its label and its colour. Returned by `GET /v1/companies/{company}/stock-reservations/statuses` as a flat list. It publishes NO permissions, and that is the answer of the domain and not an omission: the status of a reservation is not a destination a person picks — releasing and consuming stamp it.
