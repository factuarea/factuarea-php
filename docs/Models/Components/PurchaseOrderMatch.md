# PurchaseOrderMatch

The three-way match of a purchase order, read from the ORDER: what was ordered, what was actually received and what the supplier has invoiced, line by line, plus the supplier invoices linked to it. Returned by `GET /v1/companies/{company}/purchase-orders/{purchase_order}/match`. An order with no linked invoice is not an error: it answers `not_applicable` with an empty list of invoices. The line-by-line DETAIL of a deviation lives on the surface anchored to the INVOICE, which is where it is resolved.
