# TaxCatalog

The Spanish tax catalog as a single document: indirect tax regimes with their legal rates and VeriFactu code, header-level operation regimes, exemption causes with their article and legal wording, system withholding rates, and the legal VAT/equivalence-surcharge pairs. It carries no data of the authenticated company, so two different companies receive byte-identical bodies for the same language, and it is not an entity: it has no `id` and no `object`.
