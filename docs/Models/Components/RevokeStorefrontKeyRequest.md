# RevokeStorefrontKeyRequest

Optionally record why the publishable storefront credential is being revoked. `reason` (string, at most 255 chars) is optional — revoking a credential that ships inside a browser is incident response, so no reason is required to act. A blank reason is stored as none. Revocation is permanent: the credential cannot be reactivated, rotated or edited afterwards.
