# Warehouse

A warehouse: the physical place where stock lives, and the axis every movement, transfer and receipt of the ERP is booked against. It is a MASTER record, not a document: it has no number, no series and no lifecycle beyond being active or archived. Deleting one is publishing `DELETE` on it, and that ARCHIVES it — the row is kept, because three tables upstream hang their reference on it.
