# SalesOrderStatusItem

A sales order status from the closed catalog, with its label, its colour and what it allows. The three permissions are the contract that spares the integrator a round of trial and error: they say, BEFORE trying, which statuses admit editing the order, deleting it or converting it into a delivery note or an invoice.
