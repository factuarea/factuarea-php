# ReturnT

A return: the goods a customer sends back, and what has to happen with the money. It is a document of its own —it has its own number and its own lifecycle— and it hangs on the document the goods came out of. Its four transitions (approve, reject, receive, refund) are IRREVERSIBLE sub-resources with different effects, which is why there is no generic status-change operation on it: approving numbers it, receiving books the stock back in and refunding issues the corrective invoice.
