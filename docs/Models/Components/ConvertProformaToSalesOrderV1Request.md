# ConvertProformaToSalesOrderV1Request

Convert a proforma into a sales order. The body publishes ONE optional property, `target_series_id`: the public identifier of a live SALES-ORDER series of your company, which the order will be numbered with when it is confirmed; omitting it leaves the confirmation to resolve the default series of that type. There is NO partial selection of lines, and that is not an omission: the pending-per-line quantities belong to the sales order — which is what delivery notes and invoices consume from — and a proforma has no pending quantities of its own, so the conversion always carries the whole document across. The operation needs TWO scopes: write on sales orders (`sales_orders:write`), which is what the route enforces, and read on proformas (`proformas:read`), which the operation enforces on top of it. It consumes its origin: the proforma is left as converted and no published operation returns it to its previous state. Creating an order consumes monthly plan quota, and an exhausted monthly allowance answers payment required. The response is the created SALES ORDER, not the proforma.
