# CreateAutomationRuleV1RequestScope

Scope of the automation: `empresa` (the default) watches only your own company, `cartera` (only for the titular company of an account with the gestoría module) watches the other tax IDs your account operates and delivers its notices to you. Omit it to keep the current behaviour. It cannot be changed afterwards.


## Values

| Name      | Value     |
| --------- | --------- |
| `Empresa` | empresa   |
| `Cartera` | cartera   |
