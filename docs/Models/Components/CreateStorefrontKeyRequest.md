# CreateStorefrontKeyRequest

Issue a publishable storefront credential. `name` is a label to tell your credentials apart (1-120 chars). `allowed_origins` is the list of web origins the browser may use it from (1 to 10 entries, each `scheme://host[:port]`, no path, no query string, no fragment and NO WILDCARDS: neither `*` nor `https://*.example.com`) — that list is the whole access control of this credential, so every domain and subdomain is written in full. `scopes` must belong to the buyer lane (`storefront:read`, `storefront:write`); credential-management and integrator scopes are rejected. `expires_at` is optional and must be in the future. The plaintext secret is returned once in this response and never again; the prefix, the secret, its hash, the status and the company are never accepted from the body. Reaching the active-credential cap of your tier returns 422.
