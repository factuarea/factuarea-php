# WarehouseLocation

A location inside a warehouse: the aisle, the rack or the bay where the goods sit. It is a SUB-RESOURCE and never an autonomous one — it is not identified without its warehouse, and its operations consume the scope of the warehouse. The hierarchy admits ONE level: a location may hang from another, and a third level is rejected as a business rule.
