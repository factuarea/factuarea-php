# StorefrontProductSummary

One row of the shop window: an item that the merchant publishes, with what a grid or a search result needs to paint it. It is the SAME item as the full sheet, seen with fourteen keys instead of eighteen — it does not carry the variants, the presentations, the option groups or the gallery, which are four collections per row and would turn a page of twenty items into eighty collections. Whoever needs them reads the sheet of the item, or its sub-collections one by one.
