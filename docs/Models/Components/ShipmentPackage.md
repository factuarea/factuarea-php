# ShipmentPackage

One package of a shipment: a box, a pallet or a parcel, with its weight and its three dimensions. Its nine keys ALWAYS travel — `null` on the ones that are not measured — because a package with nothing filled in is a legitimate state and not a different shape. Deleting one is published as irreversible, and it verifies the company of the delivery note AND of the package before writing.
