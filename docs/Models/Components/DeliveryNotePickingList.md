# DeliveryNotePickingList

The picking list of a delivery note: the SNAPSHOT of its live lines at the moment the list was opened, with what has to be picked out of the warehouse. It has no identity of its own — it is addressed through its delivery note — and opening it again REPLACES it, losing the previous picking work, which is why the opening is published as irreversible.
