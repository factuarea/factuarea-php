# CreateStockTransferRequest

Create a stock transfer between two warehouses of your company. It is born as a DRAFT and moves no stock: the goods leave the origin when the transfer is dispatched and enter the destination when it is received. Required: `origin_warehouse_id` and `destination_warehouse_id`, two warehouses of the company in the path — one from another company answers not found, exactly like one that does not exist, and sending the same warehouse twice is rejected as a business-rule violation, because a transfer to the same place moves nothing. Optional: `notes` (up to 1000 characters) and `lines[]`, up to 100 entries. A transfer with NO lines is a legitimate draft you add lines to afterwards; what gets rejected is dispatching an empty one. Each line names the article (`product_id`, plus `product_variant_id` when one specific variant travels) and the `quantity` to dispatch, greater than zero and with at most four decimals, and may carry the location the goods leave from and the one they are placed at. The number of the transfer is stamped when it is created and is never accepted from the body; neither is the status, which the three transitions move, nor the received quantity, which only the reception moves.
