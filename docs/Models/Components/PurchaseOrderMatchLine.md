# PurchaseOrderMatchLine

One line of the order seen from the three-way match: how much was ordered, how much actually arrived and how much the supplier has invoiced. The three quantities are in the BASE unit of the item and share the same four-decimal scale, so they can be subtracted from one another without converting anything.
