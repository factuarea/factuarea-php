# DeliveryNoteShipment

The SHIPMENT of a delivery note: the carrier, the tracking and the packages the goods travel in. There is no shipment table — the shipment is the view of the transport columns of the delivery note plus its packages — so it has no identifier of its own and it is addressed through its delivery note. Its partial update touches ONLY what the body carries: an absent key is not touched, and "absent" and "set to null" are told apart explicitly and never by an empty string.
