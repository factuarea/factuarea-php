# ManagedAccountImportOwnCompanyCustomers

Outcome of the `apply_customers_to_own_company` option: the customers of `customers_file` WITHOUT `account_ref`, imported into your own company in focus (the only tax ID of the credential scope, or the only tax ID of your account). `null` when the option is off. `ERROR` `own_company_focus_required` when your account has several tax IDs and none is in focus; `ERROR` `insufficient_scope` without `contacts:write`; `ERROR` `live_credential_required` with a test key; `BLOCKED` `account_admin_required` or `operate_access_required` when the person who issued the key cannot write in that company; `BLOCKED` `nif_not_in_key_scope` when that company is outside the tax ID scope of the key; `BLOCKED` `customers_import_failed` when the contact importer failed. `unmatched_customers` counts the customers whose `account_ref` matches no row of `accounts_file`: they are imported nowhere.
