# BulkDeleteSalesOrdersRequest

Delete several sales orders in one request. `ids` is an array of 1 to 100 sales-order UUIDs; unknown or cross-tenant identifiers, and orders that cannot be deleted in their current state, are reported as failed rather than failing the whole request.
