# CreateSalesOrderRequest

Create a sales order. It is always born as a draft with no number: the number is stamped when the order is confirmed, with the sales-order series of your company. Required: `channel`, `order_date`, `buyer_name` and `lines[]` (at least one line). Optional: `expected_delivery_date`, `series_id`, `warehouse_id`, `business_contact_id`, `external_id`, `notes`, the rest of the buyer identity (`buyer_email`, `buyer_phone`, `buyer_tax_id` — at least one contact channel is needed, and the tax ID is optional because a private individual may not have one) and the two addresses, sent as flat `billing_*` and `shipping_*` fields, which the response groups under `buyer`, `billing_address` and `shipping_address`. `external_id` is the reference the system that created the order uses to identify it, and it is the key of the creation: its uniqueness scope is THE COMPANY, so sending one that is already taken is rejected naming the order that holds it, creates no second order and consumes no series number, while the same reference in two different companies is two different orders. The status, the number and the order ID are never accepted from the body, and the served, invoiced and returned quantities are never sent: they move on their own as delivery notes, invoices and returns consume the order lines. Every reference — business contact, series, warehouse, product — must belong to the company in the path: one from another company is rejected exactly like a non-existent one. Creating an order consumes monthly plan quota, and an exhausted monthly allowance answers payment required.
