# UpdateApiKeyNifScopeV1Request

Replace the scope of an API key of your account. `scoped_company_ids` is the complete resulting list of companies (company IDs, UUID v7), not a delta: at least one, all of them in your account and in the environment of the key, and never beyond the scope of the credential making the request. Every scope the key already has must fit the plan of EVERY company in the new list; otherwise the request returns 422 and nothing changes.
