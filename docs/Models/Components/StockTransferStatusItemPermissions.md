# StockTransferStatusItemPermissions

What the status admits over the transfer, answered by its state machine. The vocabulary is the one of THIS family —the three transitions plus editing— and not the one of the warehouse master: there is no `delete`, because a live transfer is CANCELLED and never erased.

| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `edit`                                                                                                                                        | *bool*                                                                                                                                        | :heavy_check_mark:                                                                                                                            | Whether the status admits adding or removing lines. Only `draft` does: once dispatched, what the document says is what left through the door. |
| `dispatch`                                                                                                                                    | *bool*                                                                                                                                        | :heavy_check_mark:                                                                                                                            | Whether the status admits dispatching the transfer.
