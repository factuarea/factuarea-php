                                                                                                                                                                                          | Where the SHIPMENT of an order is, as the buyer is shown it. The eight keys ALWAYS travel: an order with no shipment yet answers with `has_shipment` in `false`, the six data keys in `null` and `package_count` in `0`, and that is a SUCCESS and not an error — answering "not found" would confuse "there is no shipment yet" with "that order does not exist", and the buyer who has just paid would read the second one. When several shipments have gone out for the same order, what travels is the most recent one. It carries no source warehouse, no picking list, no delivery signature, no personal data of whoever received the goods, no shipping cost and not even the identifier of the delivery note: the buyer bought an order, not a delivery note, and the document of the seller is not theirs. |
