| `data`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | [Components\Promotion](../../Models/Components/Promotion.md)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | :heavy_check_mark:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | A promotion: a discount on a set of catalog items between two days of the company time zone. `starts_on` and `ends_on` are days of that zone and the last day is included; `starts_at` and `ends_at` are ISO 8601 with the offset of that zone, and `ends_at` is the last included instant. A promotion is `scheduled` until it starts, `active` while it runs, `ended` when its last day passes and `cancelled` when it was cancelled. Once it has started a promotion only admits a new `ends_on`; prices and items are frozen. |
