# PublicApiV1StripeAutoinvoicingAuthorizationsCreateResponseBody

The authorization was started. `data.url` is the Stripe link to open in the browser; `data.id` comes back as `authorization_id` in the redirect to your `return_url`. If a valid authorization of that company and account already existed it is reused: the same `id` and Stripe account come back with a fresh link. There is no `Location` header: the authorization has no individual read.
