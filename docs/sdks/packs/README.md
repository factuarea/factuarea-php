## Overview

* [publicApiV1PacksList](#publicapiv1packslist) - List all packs

## publicApiV1PacksArchive

Archive a pack: it stops being sold and disappears from the default lists, but keeps its history.
- Returns 422 `invalid_pack_status_transition` if the pack is already archived.
- Use the restore operation to bring it back.

## publicApiV1PacksCreate

Create a pack: a set of catalog products sold together at one price.
- Send the `components` (at least two) and the `pricing` rule: `fixed`, `percent_off`, `amount_off` or `sum`.
- The tax breakdown and the saving against buying the components separately are calculated for you.
- Returns 409 if the `sku` or the `slug` is already used by another product or pack.

## publicApiV1PacksList

List the packs of the company with cursor-based pagination.
- Filter by `search`, `slug` (also finds previous slugs), `sku`, `tag`, `channel`, `status`, `availability[in]` and `updated[gte]`.
- Sort with `sort` on `created`, `name` or `price` (prefix `-` for descending).
- `view=storefront` returns only the packs on sale on the web, without internal fields.
- Conditional reads: send `If-None-Match` to get `304` with no body.

### Example Usage: storefront

## publicApiV1PacksShow

Retrieve a pack by its `id`, with its price, components, gallery and, in `view=all`, its availability and internal state.

## publicApiV1PacksPriceBreakdown

Get how the price of a pack is made up: total, tax breakdown, saving and what each component contributes.
- `price_list_id`, `client_id`, `date` and `channel` price the pack for that context. The list is chosen as in a document: `price_list_id` wins (`null` = no list), else the default of `client_id` if active; an inactive `price_list_id` answers 422.
- With a promotion on that date and channel, `price` and the shares are the promotion ones and `regular_price` is the price without it.
- `warnings[]` lists what changes the result. Send `If-None-Match` to get `304`.
