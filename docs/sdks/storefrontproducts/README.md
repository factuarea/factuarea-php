## Overview

* [publicApiV1StorefrontProductsShow](#publicapiv1storefrontproductsshow) - Retrieve a storefront product

## publicApiV1StorefrontProductsList

List the articles your company publishes to its storefront, with cursor-based pagination and the same envelope as the rest of the API: the data, whether there is more and the cursor for the next page. It is authenticated with the PUBLISHABLE storefront key, the credential that travels in the browser of an anonymous shopper, so it returns only what a shopper may see: the public id, the reference, the name, the description, the categories, the base unit, the kind of item, the applicable tax, the primary image and the three flags that say whether the article has variants, presentations or options. Cost, margin, supplier, supplier offer, reorder point, per-warehouse balance and internal notes are never published here, and the list of fields is an INCLUSION list, so a new field of the article does not reach the storefront by omission. Filter by category, by reference and by whether the article has units available, sort by name, by reference or by creation date, and page with the cursor the previous page returns; a filter the contract does not declare is rejected with a validation error. An article that is not publishable is not listed, and a company in the path that is not the one that owns the key answers exactly like a company that does not exist.

## publicApiV1StorefrontProductsSearch

Search the publishable catalog of your company by free text over the name and the reference, to power the search box of a custom-built storefront. It is authenticated with the publishable storefront key that travels in the browser of an anonymous shopper. This is NOT the listing and it does NOT paginate: it answers with the best matches up to a declared ceiling of 50, which is said here on purpose, because the operation does not promise to walk the whole catalog — walking it is what the listing and its cursor are for. Every match carries the same shopper-facing fields as a row of the listing and none of the internal ones, and an article that is not publishable never appears.

### Example Usage

<!-- UsageSnippet language="php" operationID="public-api.v1.storefront.products.search" method="get" path="/companies/{company}/storefront/products/search" -->

## publicApiV1StorefrontProductsShow

Retrieve one publishable article by its public id, with everything a product page needs: the fields of a listing row plus its variants, its presentations, its option groups with their values and its images. It is authenticated with the publishable storefront key that travels in the browser of an anonymous shopper. An article that is not publishable, or that belongs to another company, answers EXACTLY like one that does not exist — anything else would reveal that it is there — and so does an article asked for under a company that is not the one that owns the key. No internal identifier, no cost, no margin, no supplier and no per-warehouse balance is ever published.

### Example Usage
