# StockTransfers.Lines

## Overview

### Available Operations

* [publicApiV1StockTransfersLinesCreate](#publicapiv1stocktransferslinescreate) - Add a line to a stock transfer
* [publicApiV1StockTransfersLinesList](#publicapiv1stocktransferslineslist) - List the lines of a stock transfer
* [publicApiV1StockTransfersLinesDelete](#publicapiv1stocktransferslinesdelete) - Remove a line from a stock transfer

## publicApiV1StockTransfersLinesCreate

Add one line to a stock transfer without resending the ones it already has: the article, its variant when it has one and the quantity to move, with four decimal places. That quantity is the one the line will dispatch, and the quantity received starts at zero. Lines can only be added while the transfer is a DRAFT: once it has been dispatched the call is rejected as a business rule violation, with 422 and never 403. Returns the line that was created, with its public id. It is not published as irreversible, because a line added to a draft is removed again with its sibling operation.

## publicApiV1StockTransfersLinesList

List the lines of a stock transfer, each one naming the article and its variant by their public id and carrying the three quantities: the one the line dispatches, the one received so far — accumulated over successive receptions — and the pending one, derived from the other two and never below zero. A transfer that has no lines yet answers with an empty list and not with an error. A transfer of another company answers as not found.

## publicApiV1StockTransfersLinesDelete

Remove one line from a stock transfer by its `id`. It only works while the transfer is a DRAFT — once dispatched the call is rejected as a business rule violation with 422 — and it is also how a line is corrected, because lines are not edited in place: remove it and add it again. Removing a line edits the transfer, it does not destroy it, so it is not published as irreversible. It returns no content.
