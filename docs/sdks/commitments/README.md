# StockAvailability.Commitments

## Overview

### Available Operations

* [publicApiV1StockAvailabilityCommitmentsList](#publicapiv1stockavailabilitycommitmentslist) - List the commitments over an article

## publicApiV1StockAvailabilityCommitmentsList

List what is currently committing stock of one article: per commitment, the reservation by its public id, its holder as a pair of type and id, the warehouse, the quantity and the expiry. It is the call that answers WHY the available balance of an article is lower than its physical balance, because the commitments listed here add up to exactly that difference. Reservations that were released, consumed or have expired are excluded: none of them is committing anything any more. An article of another company answers as not found.
