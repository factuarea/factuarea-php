# Storefront.Products.Variants

## Overview

### Available Operations

* [publicApiV1StorefrontProductsVariantsList](#publicapiv1storefrontproductsvariantslist) - List the variants of a storefront product

## publicApiV1StorefrontProductsVariantsList

List the publishable variants of an article, each one with its public id, the article it belongs to, its name, its reference, its barcode, whether it is tracked by stock and its specifications. It is authenticated with the publishable storefront key that travels in the browser of an anonymous shopper. The variants are scoped to your company AND to the publishability of the PARENT article: an article that is not publishable answers as not found and never leaks its variants through this route. A variant carries no price and no units: the price is resolved by the price operations, always on the server, and the units by the availability read.
