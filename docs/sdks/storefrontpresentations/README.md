# Storefront.Products.Presentations

## Overview

### Available Operations

* [publicApiV1StorefrontProductsPresentationsList](#publicapiv1storefrontproductspresentationslist) - List the presentations of a storefront product

## publicApiV1StorefrontProductsPresentationsList

List the publishable presentations of an article — the ways it is sold, such as a box of twelve or a 15 L drum —, each one with its public id, the article it belongs to, its name, its mode, its unit code, its conversion factor against the base unit, its nominal base quantity, its barcode and its specifications. It is authenticated with the publishable storefront key that travels in the browser of an anonymous shopper. They are scoped to your company and to the publishability of the parent article, so an article that is not publishable answers as not found. A presentation carries no price: the price of a selection that includes it is resolved on the server by the price operations, because the presentation changes the unit the price applies to.
