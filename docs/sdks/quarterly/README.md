* [publicApiV1InvoicesQuarterlySendEmail](#publicapiv1invoicesquarterlysendemail) - Email quarterly ZIP to accountant

## publicApiV1InvoicesQuarterlyAvailable

List the quarters with at least one invoice, broken down by type (F1/F2/F3/R5), plus quarters with only Stripe charges left out of invoicing.
- Those come back with `invoice_count` 0 and an empty breakdown; their ZIP holds only the charges CSV.

## publicApiV1InvoicesQuarterlyDownloadZip

Builds and streams a binary ZIP with all invoice PDFs for the requested quarter. When the company has charges left out of invoicing in that quarter and `include_non_invoiced_charges` is not `false` (default `true`), it also includes the quarterly charges/refunds CSV and per-currency totals; without such charges the file is omitted, and if the quarter has no invoices the ZIP contains only that CSV. The response reports aggregate counts in X-Quarterly-* headers; it never returns a storage path or JSON metadata payload.

## publicApiV1InvoicesQuarterlySendEmail

Generate the quarterly ZIP and email it to a recipient, typically the tax accountant.
- Unless `include_non_invoiced_charges` is `false`, it adds a CSV of charges left out of invoicing and their refunds.
- A quarter with only such charges yields a CSV-only ZIP.
